Editorial Policy & Corrections

Carl Travels is written and published by Carl Tomich, an Australian documentary filmmaker and travel creator. The site focuses on firsthand travel guides, living-abroad notes, filmmaking gear, creator workflow, and practical lessons from time spent on the road.

How Guides Are Written

Travel articles are intended to be based on places Carl has visited, filmed, researched for a trip, or lived near long enough to give practical context. When a page includes general planning information, the goal is to separate firsthand observations from details that should be checked against current official sources.

Dates, Prices, Rules, and Sources

Travel information changes quickly. Prices, visa rules, opening hours, transport routes, and entry conditions should be treated as planning guidance, not a guarantee. Pages are reviewed and updated when errors are found, when a destination changes, or when a guide is prepared for a new season.

Reviews and Affiliate Links

Gear and service recommendations should explain why a product or service is useful, who it suits, and what the drawbacks are. Some links may be affiliate links or sponsored links. Affiliate relationships do not change the price paid by the reader and are disclosed on relevant pages and in the Affiliate Disclosure.

Corrections

If something is inaccurate, outdated, unclearly sourced, or missing important context, email carl@music-records.com.au with the page URL and the issue. Corrections are reviewed manually and updated on the page when appropriate.

Medical, Legal, and Financial Topics

Some articles discuss travel-adjacent topics such as dental tourism, visas, citizenship paperwork, insurance, money cards, or cost of living. These pages are personal or practical travel content and are not a substitute for professional medical, legal, immigration, tax, or financial advice.
