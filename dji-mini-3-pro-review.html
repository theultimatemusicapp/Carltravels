DJI Mini 3 Pro Review

More than just a drone.

Welcome back to Carl Tomich Tech Reviews! Today we’re looking at a drone I’ve come to love — and not just for flying. The DJI Mini 3 Pro is one of the most versatile tools in my kit, and I want to show you why it’s not just for aerial footage.

🎥 The Drone That Doubles as a Camera

Yep, you read that right. I’ve been using the Mini 3 Pro as a handheld camera — and with its built-in gimbal and stabilized footage, it holds up surprisingly well.

You can literally hold the drone by hand, walk with it, tilt and pan — and it gives you buttery smooth footage. It’s like having a pocket-sized Steadicam. This is a great hack if you're traveling light or don’t want to carry an extra gimbal.

🧊 The Cooling Quirk: Mini 2 vs Mini 3 Pro

There’s one thing DJI changed that’s worth knowing — especially if you’re using the drone as a handheld cam or keeping it powered on while stationary:

  • DJI Mini 2 had a built-in cooling fan, so it could sit powered on for over an hour, even without flying.
  • DJI Mini 3 Pro removed that fan to make room for more sensors.

The result? The Mini 3 Pro overheats and auto-shuts down if it’s just sitting idle for too long — usually around 5 to 10 minutes depending on the weather.

Pro Tip: If you’re planning to use it as a static or handheld camera, just put a small fan on it to keep it cool. Yep, it’s janky — but it works.

✈️ Still a Great Drone for Flying Too

Let’s not forget — this is still a fantastic drone in the air:

  • 4K video at up to 60fps
  • Vertical shooting mode (perfect for reels and shorts)
  • Obstacle sensors on three sides
  • Lightweight enough to fly without registration in many countries

It’s small, powerful, and incredibly versatile — especially for travel creators, vloggers, and content pros.
