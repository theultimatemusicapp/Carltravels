Destination Guide

Far North Queensland

Where the Reef meets the Rainforest. Explore Cairns, Port Douglas, and beyond.

Explore Guides Watch Videos

The Tropics Await

Cairns is more than just a gateway; it's the heartbeat of tropical Australia. From the prehistoric Daintree Rainforest to the vibrant underwater world of the Great Barrier Reef, Far North Queensland offers an adventure for every soul.

Exploring Port Douglas

Just an hour from Cairns, Port Douglas is your gateway to the Reef and Rainforest. Stroll Four Mile Beach or dine on Macrossan Street.

Green Island vs. Fitzroy Island

Which island should you choose? Green Island offers family fun and a coral cay, while Fitzroy boasts hiking trails and Nudey Beach.

FNQ Highlights

  • Great Barrier Reef: Snorkel or dive the world's largest coral reef.
  • Daintree Rainforest: Earth's oldest surviving rainforest.
  • Port Douglas: Upscale dining and Four Mile Beach.
  • Kuranda: Scenic railway and skyrail adventures.
  • Atherton Tablelands: Waterfalls, lakes, and food trails.

Practical Info

Best Time to Visit

May to October (Dry Season) for mild weather and swimming. November to April (Wet Season) for lush rainforests.

Getting Around

Renting a car is best for exploring beyond Cairns. Shuttle buses connect Cairns to Port Douglas.
