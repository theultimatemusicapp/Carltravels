Vietnam Theme Park Escape

VinWonders Nha Trang

Hop the Vinpearl cable car to Vietnam's most spectacular island playground. From fairy-tale castles and looping coasters to sunset Ferris wheel views, VinWonders is the definition of a full-throttle Nha Trang adventure.

Watch the Experience Plan Your Visit
Featured Episode

Discover VinWonders from Above

Glide across Nha Trang Bay on one of the world's longest over-water cable cars, then dive into a cinematic tour of Vietnam's largest theme park. Castles, roller coasters, water slides, and golden-hour skyline views await.

Sky-High Arrival

Start your day floating 3.3 km across the sea. The Vinpearl cable car delivers sweeping views of Hon Tre Island, Nha Trang's coastline, and the surrounding mountains , an unforgettable entrance before you even scan your ticket.

Castles & Fantasy Worlds

VinWonders' fairy-tale zones are filled with immersive theming: wander through European-style palaces, catch 4D shows, and snap portraits under the turrets and stained-glass windows that feel straight out of a storybook.

Thrills & Water Slides

Chase adrenaline on looping coasters, tower-drop rides, and the iconic Sky Wheel. When the tropical heat kicks in, cool off next door at the water park with lazy rivers, wave pools, and splash zones for every age.

Why VinWonders Is a Must-Visit

VinWonders is more than a theme park , it’s an island resort packed into one destination. Spend the morning conquering Adventure Land’s roller coasters, wander through World Garden’s themed biospheres, and catch cultural performances in the amphitheater as the sun dips behind the bay.

Tickets start around 800,000 VND for full-day access, with half-price evening passes after 4 PM. Students and children enjoy additional discounts, and combo passes with Vinpearl hotels make it easy to turn your visit into a weekend getaway.

Stick around for sunset when the Ferris wheel lights up the skyline. Nightly fountain shows, street food stalls, and live music keep the energy going until closing time , perfect for capturing cinematic footage for your own Vietnam travel reel.

Quick Facts

Location

VinWonders (Vinpearl Land), Hon Tre Island, Nha Trang, Vietnam

Tickets

From 800,000 VND (half-price after 4 PM). Student & child discounts available.

Best Time

Arrive by late morning, stay through sunset for the Ferris wheel and fountain shows.

Getting There

Grab a taxi or ride-share to the Vinpearl cable car terminal, then sail over the bay in 12 minutes.

Must-Shoot Spots

Castle courtyard, Sky Wheel, Fairy Land bridge, water park lagoon, sunset cable car ride.

Plan Your Day

How to Maximise VinWonders

  • Beat the crowds by arriving when gates open. Head straight to Adventure Land and Dragon's Run coaster before midday lines build.
  • Pack swimwear and a quick-dry towel so you can jump between the water park and theme park zones without heading back to lockers.
  • Grab lunch inside World Garden’s covered food court. Local Vietnamese dishes, seafood bowls, and family-friendly options keep energy levels high.
  • Stay for the evening show at the Musical Fountain Plaza. It’s timed perfectly with sunset and offers a front-row seat to the bay’s pastel skyline.
  • Download the VinWonders app for wait times, show schedules, and interactive maps so you never miss a parade or performance.
Perfect Pairings

Make It a Nha Trang Weekend

Pair VinWonders with an island-hopping day trip to Hon Mun, or slow down with a sunrise coffee along Tran Phu Beach. For nightlife, the Sailing Club and rooftop bars downtown keep the energy high once you’re back on the mainland.

Nearby Highlights

  • Hon Chong Rocks
  • Nha Trang Cathedral
  • Long Son Pagoda

Family Tips

  • Measure kids at entry for height bands
  • Use shaded stroller parking near Fairy Land
  • Break for indoor shows during midday heat
