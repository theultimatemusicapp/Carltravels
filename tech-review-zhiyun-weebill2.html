Steady Shots With the Weebill 2

The Weebill 2 is a compact 3-axis gimbal that handles mirrorless cameras with ease. I’ve hauled it through crowded markets and rocky trails with reliable results.

Why It Works

  • Responsive touchscreen for quick settings
  • Strong motors keep footage steady with heavier lenses
  • Underslung mode for low-angle shots

Limitations

Battery life is average and balancing can be fiddly at first. Once dialed in, it performs beautifully.

Overall

For travellers who want silky smooth footage without a huge rig, the Weebill 2 is a solid choice.
