Sony A7S III Review

The ultimate low-light travel camera.

The Sony A7S III is famous among travel filmmakers for its unmatched low-light performance and silky 4K footage. After using it on shoots from Berlin to Bali, here are my quick impressions.

Key Features

  • 12MP full-frame sensor for incredible low-light video
  • 4K recording up to 120fps with 10-bit color
  • Flip-out LCD and blazing fast autofocus
  • Excellent dynamic range for grading

Why I Love It

Whether I’m shooting night markets in Hanoi or dimly lit clubs in Berlin, the A7S III keeps footage clean and detailed. The colors straight out of camera are gorgeous, and the image stabilisation helps for handheld run‑and‑gun filming.

Where It Falls Short

It isn’t cheap and the 12MP photos won’t satisfy dedicated photographers. For purely travel snaps, I still pack a small secondary camera or my phone.

Verdict

If video is your priority and you need dependable low-light performance, the A7S III remains one of the best tools out there. It’s pricey, but it has never let me down on the road.
