Core Features

  • AI Upscaling & Super Resolution: upscale images up to 16K or 32K with detail reconstruction instead of basic pixel stretch.
  • Noise Reduction: clean low-light grain while preserving edges and texture.
  • Deblurring & Detail Restoration: sharpen soft images and recover detail in architecture, portraits, and landscapes.
  • Face Restoration: improve facial detail for blurred or pixelated portrait shots.
  • Artifact Removal: reduce blocky compression artifacts from web or messaging app images.
  • Batch Processing: process large image sets in one run for production workflows.
  • GPU Acceleration: supports NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel hardware for faster renders.

Pricing

  • Lifetime License: around $99–$155 one-time (promo dependent).
  • Free Trial: available with limited features.
  • Support: includes a 30-day money-back guarantee.

The lifetime option can be more cost-effective for creators who enhance images regularly.

Why It Stands Out

  1. One-time purchase option compared with subscription-heavy alternatives.
  2. Very high upscaling limits for print and large-format digital use.
  3. Natural detail output with limited artifacting in many real-world cases.
  4. Strong batch workflow support for teams and large image libraries.
  5. Hardware optimization for faster local processing.
  6. Simple workflow that does not require deep manual tuning.

Limitations

Some users report occasional color shifts, and the app is focused on enhancement rather than full manual image editing (cropping, layered retouching, and deep adjustments).

Conclusion

Aiarty AI Image Enhancer is a practical choice for photographers, designers, and creators who need fast quality improvements on low-resolution or imperfect images. The combination of strong enhancement models, batch processing, and lifetime pricing makes it a compelling alternative in the AI upscaling category.
