AIARTY vs Topaz Video AI (2026): Which Video Enhancer Wins?

Short answer: if you want the best possible detail at extreme upscales, Topaz still edges it. If you want faster renders, a cleaner workflow, and a lower price, AIARTY is the better daily driver. I tested both on travel footage, old 1080p clips, and noisy night shots to see which one I’d actually use when I’m on deadline.

My real-world test setup

  • Machine: RTX 4070 laptop GPU, 32 GB RAM
  • Footage: 2016 travel clips (1080p), noisy night street shots, and 30fps GoPro clips
  • Targets: 4K exports for YouTube, 60fps interpolations for smooth motion
  • Goal: clean detail without the “plastic face” look or motion shimmer

I used AIARTY’s MoDetail and Denoise presets, then matched them as closely as possible in Topaz using Proteus + Iris with light grain recovery.

Quality notes (what I actually saw)

AIARTY keeps skin tones more natural and avoids harsh edge halos. It’s not as sharp as Topaz on tiny details, but it feels more “real” for YouTube uploads where compression will eat detail anyway.

Topaz Video AI wins on micro-detail and heavy restoration. Old footage looks crisper, but it’s easier to over-sharpen and get flicker in backgrounds. You need to babysit settings more.

My takeaway: AIARTY is better for fast turnarounds, Topaz is better if you’re selling restoration work or delivering masters to clients.

Pros and cons

AIARTY Pros

  • Cleaner UI and easier batch workflow
  • More affordable lifetime license

AIARTY Cons

  • Less micro-detail on extreme upscales
  • Fewer advanced tuning controls

Topaz Pros

  • Best-in-class detail recovery
  • More models for niche restoration work

Topaz Cons

  • Higher price and paid upgrades
  • Longer render times
  • More trial-and-error to avoid artifacts

Verdict

If you’re a creator who needs solid results quickly, AIARTY is the one I’d keep installed. If you’re restoring old films or charging clients for premium restoration, Topaz Video AI still has the edge. I use AIARTY for 80% of my work and pull out Topaz only when I need that last 10% of detail.

FAQ

Is AIARTY a good Topaz Video AI alternative?

Yes, especially for creators who prioritize speed and cost over extreme detail recovery.

Which is better for 1080p to 4K upscaling?

Topaz wins on micro-detail, but AIARTY looks more natural once YouTube compression hits.

Does AIARTY handle noise better than Topaz?

In my tests AIARTY kept shadows cleaner, while Topaz sharpened more but introduced flicker if pushed.

Which one is easier to use?

AIARTY. The presets are faster to dial in and batch processing is simpler.

Do I need both?

Only if you’re doing client restoration work. For everyday creator workflows, one tool is enough.
