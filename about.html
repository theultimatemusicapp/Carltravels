About Carl Tomich

Biography

Carl Tomich is an Australian documentary filmmaker and travel creator known for Busking for Berlin, Busk Life and his travel films documenting life abroad.

I am from Perth, Western Australia, and my path into film came through music, live performance, video production, and years of making work from unstable situations rather than clean studio conditions. I have worked behind the camera for more than 14 years across documentary, travel, online media, editing, and creator-led video.

After starting in Perth and spending time in Melbourne, I moved to Berlin in 2011. I lived there for approximately a decade, busked with a guitar, filmed artists around the city, and directed Busking for Berlin, a documentary built from the street-level reality of trying to survive through music.

I later made Busk Life, returning to the busking world years later with a different kind of perspective. My current work includes documentaries, travel films, practical location guides, gear reviews, and video projects produced internationally under the Carl Travels brand.

A lot of my work places me directly inside the subject I am documenting: living from busking income, rebuilding a life abroad, following transport routes myself, or training in martial arts as part of a long-form documentary process. I am less interested in polished travel fantasy than in what actually happens when a person leaves the known version of their life and has to build the next one in public.

INSIDE THE WORK

Documenting from the Middle of It

My documentaries and travel films often use direct participation as part of the method. I do not only interview people from the outside; I put myself into the route, the street, the training room, the airport transfer, or the uncomfortable life change and film from there.

That approach runs through the Berlin busking films, the travel guides on this site, and current documentary work built around movement, discipline, uncertainty, and creative survival.
