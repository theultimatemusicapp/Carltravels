Hanoi Essentials 2025

Top 10 Things to Do in Hanoi, Vietnam

By Carl Tomich | Last reviewed August 2, 2026 | Editorial policy

Welcome to the capital where chaos meets charm. I’m Carl, and together with Nhi , our Hanoi local , we’ve distilled the city’s pulse into ten unforgettable experiences designed for travellers who want to feel the real Vietnam.

Watch the Travel Guide Jump to the Top 10

Where Hanoi’s Heartbeat Never Stops

Hanoi hits you in the best way possible. It’s old and new, chaotic and calm, all at once. From Train Street’s thunderous rush to the soft chants floating across West Lake, every moment shifts gears. This guide isn’t built for influencers chasing the same angles , it’s for curious travellers ready to walk, eat, ride, and live the city the way locals do.

Use this 2025 travel blueprint to plan sunrise markets, midnight beer runs, and day trips that stretch into the northern highlands. Each spot below is backed by firsthand experience from our Globe Travel Adventures crew on the ground.

How I Would Use This List

Treat this as a shortlist, not a race. Hanoi is better when you group nearby stops and leave space for food, traffic, heat, and the small surprises that happen between the famous sights.

  • Old Quarter day: Train Street, the Old Quarter, Dong Xuan Market, and Beer Street fit naturally together if you are comfortable walking and using short Grab rides.
  • West Lake day: Tran Quoc Pagoda, Truc Bach, Lotte Mall West Lake, and the Botanical Gardens make a slower route with more breathing room.
  • Early morning option: Quang Ba Flower Market only makes sense if you are willing to start before dawn; otherwise save your energy for the markets and food streets later in the day.
Field Notes

Before You Go

  • Transport: Build each day around two or three areas rather than zig-zagging across the city.
  • Weather: Hanoi heat and rain can change the plan quickly, so keep one indoor backup such as Lotte Mall or a cafe stop.
  • Etiquette: At temples and pagodas, dress modestly, keep voices low, and avoid treating worship spaces as photo sets.
  • Safety: Train Street access can change. Follow local instructions on the day and do not stand on the tracks for photos.

City Checklist

The Must-Do Hanoi Hit List

From alleys shaking under train horns to lotus ponds glowing at dusk, here’s how to stack ten experiences into a perfectly paced Hanoi itinerary. Mix-and-match these picks to fit 48 hours or a full week.

1️⃣ Train Street

The Chaos That Defines Hanoi

Grab a condensed milk coffee, listen for the horn, and feel the rumble inches from your knees. It’s not just a photo-op , it’s Hanoi adapting in real time.

2️⃣ The Old Quarter

1,000 Years of History in Motion

Wander streets named after ancient trades, dodge scooters, and peek at French balconies above heritage shophouses that still hum with family businesses.

3️⃣ Beer Street (Tạ Hiện)

Plastic Stools, Neon Nights

When the sun drops, the city’s social heart explodes with draft beer, grilled skewers, and conversations that stretch into the early morning.

4️⃣ Quảng Bá Flower Market

Hanoi Before Sunrise

Show up at 3 a.m. for the jasmine-scented hustle. Watch vendors unload fresh blooms while the city rubs the sleep from its eyes.

5️⃣ Đồng Xuân Market

The Wholesale Lifeline

Built in 1889 and still unstoppable , fabrics, spices, souvenirs, and a weekend night market that spills into the Old Quarter.

6️⃣ Imperial Citadel of Thăng Long

A Millennium of Power

UNESCO-listed grounds layered with royal palaces and wartime bunkers. Stand still and feel the weight of a thousand years beneath your feet.

7️⃣ Trấn Quốc Pagoda

Peace on West Lake

Visit at sunset for candlelight reflections, chanting monks, and a rare pocket of calm in the capital.

8️⃣ Hanoi Botanical Gardens

The City’s Calm Corner

Sneak behind the Presidential Palace for banyan shade, bird song, and a breather when the traffic gets wild.

9️⃣ Lotte Mall West Lake

Hanoi’s Modern Face

Slip into sleek cafés, cinemas, and the Lotte World Aquarium before catching golden-hour views from the rooftop terrace.

🔟 Trúc Bạch Lake & Hà Giang Loop

Where the Journey Begins

Rent a swan paddle boat, sip coffee lakeside, then gear up for the Hà Giang Loop , the three-day ride that unlocks Vietnam’s wild north.
