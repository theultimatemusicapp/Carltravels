City Checklist

The Must-Do Hanoi Hit List

From alleys shaking under train horns to lotus ponds glowing at dusk, here’s how to stack ten experiences into a perfectly paced Hanoi itinerary. Mix-and-match these picks to fit 48 hours or a full week.

1️⃣ Train Street

The Chaos That Defines Hanoi

Grab a condensed milk coffee, listen for the horn, and feel the rumble inches from your knees. It’s not just a photo-op — it’s Hanoi adapting in real time.

2️⃣ The Old Quarter

1,000 Years of History in Motion

Wander streets named after ancient trades, dodge scooters, and peek at French balconies above heritage shophouses that still hum with family businesses.

3️⃣ Beer Street (Tạ Hiện)

Plastic Stools, Neon Nights

When the sun drops, the city’s social heart explodes with draft beer, grilled skewers, and conversations that stretch into the early morning.

4️⃣ Quảng Bá Flower Market

Hanoi Before Sunrise

Show up at 3 a.m. for the jasmine-scented hustle. Watch vendors unload fresh blooms while the city rubs the sleep from its eyes.

5️⃣ Đồng Xuân Market

The Wholesale Lifeline

Built in 1889 and still unstoppable — fabrics, spices, souvenirs, and a weekend night market that spills into the Old Quarter.

6️⃣ Imperial Citadel of Thăng Long

A Millennium of Power

UNESCO-listed grounds layered with royal palaces and wartime bunkers. Stand still and feel the weight of a thousand years beneath your feet.

7️⃣ Trấn Quốc Pagoda

Peace on West Lake

Visit at sunset for candlelight reflections, chanting monks, and a rare pocket of calm in the capital.

8️⃣ Hanoi Botanical Gardens

The City’s Calm Corner

Sneak behind the Presidential Palace for banyan shade, bird song, and a breather when the traffic gets wild.

9️⃣ Lotte Mall West Lake

Hanoi’s Modern Face

Slip into sleek cafés, cinemas, and the Lotte World Aquarium before catching golden-hour views from the rooftop terrace.

🔟 Trúc Bạch Lake & Hà Giang Loop

Where the Journey Begins

Rent a swan paddle boat, sip coffee lakeside, then gear up for the Hà Giang Loop — the three-day ride that unlocks Vietnam’s wild north.
