THE JOURNEY

Leaving Everything Behind

I turned 40 in a country that no longer felt like home. The bars closed. The rules multiplied. The life I was living started to feel smaller every year. So I left.

Not because I thought moving would fix everything. But because staying felt like giving up.

This is the story of what happens when you walk away from the safe option and try to build something real.

TRAINING & DISCIPLINE

The Physical Rebuild

Empty gyms at 6am. Muay Thai pads in cheap apartments. Sleep struggles. Mental lows. The slow, unglamorous work of rebuilding a body and mind that spent too long sitting still.

Training isn't about transformation photos. It's about showing up when you don't want to. It's the only thing that makes sense when everything else is chaos.

THE WORK

Building Something Real

Fourteen years behind a camera. Editing in cafes. Filming empty streets at 3am. Uploading stock footage and hoping it sells. Building YouTube channels that might pay rent next month.

This is what creative work actually looks like when you strip away the Instagram version. Late nights. Tight budgets. Obsessive attention to detail. And the quiet satisfaction of making something that matters.

No Bullshit. No Guru. No Course.

I'm not here to sell you freedom. I'm not financially free. I'm not living my best life. I'm not going to teach you how to quit your job and travel the world.

I'm just a filmmaker trying to build something honest while moving between countries. Some months I make decent money. Some months I don't. I train because it keeps me sane. I film because it's the only thing I know how to do.

If you're looking for a blueprint, you won't find it here. If you're looking for someone who's still figuring it out and willing to document the messy parts, then stick around.
