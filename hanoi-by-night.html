Hanoi by Night

A cinematic short film capturing the contrasts of Hanoi after dark — from the neon glow of Beer Street to the stillness of Ngọc Sơn Temple.

Time marker: filmed between 11:30 PM and 2:00 AM. Money marker: about $12 on street snacks and coffee. Trade-off/regret: I skipped sunrise B-roll to protect the night-only mood.

Article Section

Problem → Decision → Outcome

A quick blueprint for how the night film came together, written for anyone planning a short, atmospheric shoot.

Problem

Midnight streets are gorgeous, but the light drops fast and crowds move unpredictably.

I needed a tight route that still felt cinematic without rushing every frame.

Mini-hook: the route fix is coming next.

Decision

I committed to three zones: Old Quarter alleys, Hoàn Kiếm Lake, and Beer Street.

Every stop had one hero shot and one ambient cutaway before moving on.

Mini-hook: wait for the payoff at the lake.

Outcome

The sequence flows like a night walk and keeps the pacing intentional.

It also left enough energy to capture the calm after the chaos.

The Midnight Spirit of Hanoi

This is one of the projects I’m most proud of so far. Filmed around midnight in Hanoi’s Old Quarter, Hoàn Kiếm Lake, and Beer Street, it captures the city in all its contrasts.

Think chaos in the crowds, silence at Ngọc Sơn Temple, and neon glow that makes the sidewalks shimmer.

For the daylight version of this energy, start with my top 10 things to do in Hanoi checklist.

Mid-Article Check-in

Time marker: 1:15 AM halfway point at Hoàn Kiếm Lake. Money marker: $4 for hot trà đá and a late-night snack. Trade-off/regret: I skipped a second pass through Train Street and felt it later.

Mini-hook: the next paragraph explains why the trade-off still worked.

I’ve always wanted to show Hanoi not just as a travel destination, but as an experience — a city that feels alive long after midnight.

This short film is my attempt at creating something more timeless: a cinematic piece that reflects the true spirit of Vietnam at night.

I’d love to hear your thoughts — does this capture the Hanoi you know, or the one you dream of visiting?
