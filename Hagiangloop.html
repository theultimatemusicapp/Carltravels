Hà Giang Loop (3D2N) – Easy Rider Motorbike Tour from Hanoi

By Carl Tomich | Last reviewed August 2, 2026 | Editorial policy

Northern Vietnam’s ultimate road trip: terrace valleys, karst plateaus, cliff-edge mountain roads, deep river canyons, pine forests, village life, sunrise coffee, and starry nights. We based with Bông Hostel / Bong Vietnam Tours and chose the Easy Rider option (personal drivers) for a stress-free, camera-in-hand ride.

3 Days / 2 Nights Easy Rider Drivers Karst Plateaus Bông Hostel Base
Watch the Loop Recap Book with Bông Hostel

Tour Snapshot

  • Easy Rider (pillion) or self-ride semi-auto bike options
  • Two unforgettable homestay nights with family-style dinners
  • Support truck and bike insurance included
  • Free pre-loop hostel night plus bus coordination

How to Get to Hà Giang

Bus tickets are available from Hanoi Old Quarter, Sapa, Ninh Binh, Cát Bà, and Hạ Long with pickup the day before the tour. Every booking includes a free pre-tour night in a dorm at Bông Hà Giang Hostel so you can rest, meet your guides, and repack for the mountains.

Bus Booking Tips

  • Reserve sleeper, cabin, or limousine buses directly through Bông Hostel.
  • Meet at the hostel the evening prior for paperwork and permit guidance.
  • Arriving from elsewhere? Message the team for coordinated pickup windows.

Pre-Tour Night

  • Free dorm bed included; private rooms available for an extra fee.
  • Secure luggage storage for city gear you don’t need on the loop.
  • Short briefing covers weather, safety, and the support vehicle.

Need-to-Know

  • Bring your passport for the mandatory Hà Giang travel permit (sorted on-site).
  • Weather shifts fast: pack light rain gear and warm evening layers.
  • Carry cash for coffee stops, market snacks, and the permit fee.

What’s Included (3D2N)

Easy Rider or Self-Ride

Local guide/driver (Easy Rider) or semi-auto bike (110cc) with fuel if you’re riding yourself.

Peace of Mind

Bike insurance plus a support vehicle/team ready when the terrain or weather gets challenging.

Homestay Hospitality

Two community homestay nights (dorm style) with hearty breakfasts and family dinners.

Meals & Tickets

Three breakfasts, three lunches, and two dinners plus all sightseeing tickets on the loop.

Bonus Hostel Night

Enjoy a free night in Hà Giang before departure; opt for a private room upgrade for a small fee.

Not Included

Drinks, personal expenses, and the Hà Giang travel permit (payable on-site at Bông Hostel).

Typical 3D2N Flow

Day 1 , Loop Launch

Start with breakfast at the hostel around 8:00, a quick gear check, then a 10:00 departure. Expect terrace valleys, pine S-bends, ridge lookouts, and a family-style dinner at your first homestay. New friends guaranteed.

Base: Bông Hà Giang Hostel

Day 2 , Canyon Highs

Ride the high karst plateau, tackle a cliff-edge pass above a deep river canyon, and chase golden hour through the limestone peaks. Wind down with a campfire, border-ridge stars, and stories from the Easy Rider crew.

Base: Bông Hà Giang Hostel

Day 3 , Sunrise to Send-Off

Wake for sunrise coffee, dive into a buzzing local market, and savour the final S-bends before rolling back into Hà Giang. Freshen up at Bông Hostel and let the team book your onward bus south.

Base: Bông Hà Giang Hostel

Bus & Bike Upgrades

  • Bông can arrange sleeper, cabin, or limousine buses to match your comfort level.
  • Upgrade to manual bikes (YB125 or XR150) if you’re confident on mountain switchbacks.
  • Luggage transfers available so you can ride with only the essentials.

Easy Rider vs Self-Ride

We opted for Easy Rider so we could stay camera-ready, soak in the scenery, and capture drone footage while the pros handled hairpin turns. If you self-ride, be confident managing narrow, winding mountain roads and confirm your license plus travel insurance cover motorbikes in Vietnam.

Pack List Essentials

  • Light rain shell and warm mid-layer for windy passes.
  • Closed shoes, gloves, and sunscreen for long ride days.
  • Water bottle plus cash for roadside coffee and markets.
  • Portable charger to keep phones and cameras alive.

FAQ

  • Do I need to ride myself?
    No,Easy Rider drivers are available so you can ride pillion.
  • Is it safe?
    The team adjusts the route for weather; listen to your guide and dress for quick temperature swings.
  • Best time to go?
    Dry months are most popular, but micro-climates happen year-round,pack layers regardless.
