Why Every Traveller Should Get a Wise Card

When you're travelling, every little hidden fee adds up. Whether it’s dodgy airport exchange counters, bad ATM rates, or your bank sneakily charging you 3% every time you tap your card abroad,money leaks happen faster than you realize. After years of travelling across dozens of countries, I can honestly say: getting a Wise card was one of the best financial moves I ever made for travel. Here’s everything you need to know about it,and why you should set one up before your next trip.

What is Wise?

Wise (formerly TransferWise) is an international money service designed for modern travellers, freelancers, remote workers, and basically anyone who moves money across borders. Their Wise debit card is connected to a multi-currency account where you can:

  • Hold 40+ currencies.
  • Spend anywhere in the world at real exchange rates.
  • Avoid hidden fees that banks or credit cards normally charge.

You just top up your account online and spend like a local, wherever you are.

Why I Recommend Wise for Travellers

Here’s why Wise has become my #1 travel finance tool:

  • Best Exchange Rates: You get the real mid-market exchange rate (the one you see on Google), not a marked-up tourist rate.
  • No Hidden Fees: Wise tells you exactly what you’ll pay,and it’s often 70% cheaper than using a regular bank.
  • Free ATM Withdrawals: You can withdraw cash for free up to a certain amount (usually around $350–$400 AUD monthly, depending on your country).
  • Multi-Currency Accounts: Hold USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, JPY, and more,all in one account.
  • Instant Currency Switching: Switch between currencies instantly inside the app when the rate is good.
  • Real-Time Spending Alerts: Get instant notifications every time you use your card.
  • Prepaid Top-Up: You can only spend what you have. No surprises, no debt.
  • Virtual Card Available: For even more secure online purchases while travelling.

Real-World Example

When I spent months travelling across Vietnam, Japan, Germany, France, and Albania, I used my Wise card everywhere:

  • Paid for hotels in EUR without my home bank slapping me with a 3% foreign fee.
  • Withdrew VND in Vietnam at local ATMs without crazy withdrawal charges.
  • Swapped AUD to JPY instantly while standing in line for ramen in Tokyo.

It saved me hundreds of dollars compared to using a traditional bank card.

How to Get a Wise Card

Setting it up is easy:

  1. Create an account online (it takes 5 minutes).
  2. Verify your identity (passport or driver's license).
  3. Order your Wise card (delivery is usually quick, depending on your country).
  4. Start loading money and travelling smarter.

💡 Note: Delivery times and free ATM withdrawal limits may vary by country. Check Wise’s website for details.
