Phu Quoc Travel Guide 2026
Where To Stay, When To Go & What To See

I landed in Phu Quoc last minute with zero plan and started in Sunset Town at night. This page covers the key things most people ask first: best areas to stay, weather timing, top attractions, practical tips, and whether the island's big-ticket experiences are actually worth it.

Best Areas To Stay

  • Duong Dong / Long Beach: Best all-round base for first-timers, sunset bars, restaurants, and easy airport access.
  • Sunset Town (An Thoi): Great if you want cable car access, night shows, and dramatic architecture; quieter in daytime, livelier after dark.
  • Ong Lang: More relaxed and green; good for slower trips and boutique stays.
  • Northwest coast: Resort-heavy and calmer, ideal for couples or full resort days.

Best Time Of Year

  • Nov-Apr: Best weather window (dry season), lower rain risk, clear sunsets and better island-hopping days.
  • May-Oct: Rainy season with short heavy showers; still possible to travel but keep plans flexible.
  • Shoulder months: Late Oct/early Nov and April can offer better prices with decent weather.

Top Attractions

  • Sunset Town night walk, bridge area, and evening shows.
  • Hon Thom Cable Car (world's longest over-sea cable car).
  • Sun World Nature Park rides + water park.
  • Sao Beach and Khem Beach for calmer water and beach days.
  • Night market seafood in Duong Dong.

Practical Tips

  • Use Grab where available, but consider a scooter if you're confident in traffic.
  • For Sun World day trips, go early to avoid heat and peak queues.
  • Bring reef-safe sunscreen, water shoes, and a dry bag for boat/cable car days.
  • Mobile data helps a lot for transport and translation , Saily eSIM with code CARL10 is included in the video notes.

What I Would Budget

Phu Quoc can be cheap if you stay local and keep transport simple, but it is not the cheapest part of Vietnam. Island logistics push prices up. A comfortable mid-range trip is usually built around a basic hotel or apartment, Grab rides or scooter hire, local seafood meals, and one or two paid attractions.

  • Simple room or apartment: often around 500,000-1,000,000 VND per night depending on area and season.
  • Local meal: roughly 60,000-150,000 VND; Western food and resort restaurants cost much more.
  • Scooter rental: usually cheaper than repeated taxi rides if you are confident, licensed, and insured.
  • Sun World / cable car day: treat it as a proper paid day out, not a tiny side activity.

Transport Reality

The island is larger than it looks on a map. Sunset Town, Duong Dong, Ong Lang, and the northern resort areas are not quick walks from each other. If you stay in the wrong area for your plans, you will spend half the trip sitting in cars or negotiating scooter rides. For a short first visit, I would choose either Duong Dong / Long Beach for convenience or Sunset Town if the cable car and night architecture are the whole point of the trip.

Who Phu Quoc Is For

Phu Quoc works best for beach breaks, couples, families, resort travellers, and people who want an easy island add-on after Hanoi, Da Nang, or Ho Chi Minh City. It is less ideal if you want dense street life, walkable neighbourhoods, or the cultural depth you get in older Vietnamese cities.

Mistakes to Avoid

Do not book the cheapest place you find without checking the location. A bargain room far from food, transport, and beaches can become expensive fast. I would also avoid planning every day around the weather. In rainy season, leave space in the schedule and keep the big paid day flexible.

If you are building a longer Vietnam route, pair this with my Hanoi guide, Da Nang guide, and Vietnam e-visa guide so the island part fits the rest of the trip instead of becoming an expensive detour.
