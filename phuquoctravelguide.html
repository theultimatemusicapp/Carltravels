Phu Quoc Travel Guide 2026
Where To Stay, When To Go & What To See

I landed in Phu Quoc last minute with zero plan and started in Sunset Town at night. This page covers the key things most people ask first: best areas to stay, weather timing, top attractions, practical tips, and whether the island's big-ticket experiences are actually worth it.

Best Areas To Stay

  • Duong Dong / Long Beach: Best all-round base for first-timers, sunset bars, restaurants, and easy airport access.
  • Sunset Town (An Thoi): Great if you want cable car access, night shows, and dramatic architecture; quieter in daytime, livelier after dark.
  • Ong Lang: More relaxed and green; good for slower trips and boutique stays.
  • Northwest coast: Resort-heavy and calmer, ideal for couples or full resort days.

Best Time Of Year

  • Nov-Apr: Best weather window (dry season), lower rain risk, clear sunsets and better island-hopping days.
  • May-Oct: Rainy season with short heavy showers; still possible to travel but keep plans flexible.
  • Shoulder months: Late Oct/early Nov and April can offer better prices with decent weather.

Top Attractions

  • Sunset Town night walk, bridge area, and evening shows.
  • Hon Thom Cable Car (world's longest over-sea cable car).
  • Sun World Nature Park rides + water park.
  • Sao Beach and Khem Beach for calmer water and beach days.
  • Night market seafood in Duong Dong.

Practical Tips

  • Use Grab where available, but consider a scooter if you're confident in traffic.
  • For Sun World day trips, go early to avoid heat and peak queues.
  • Bring reef-safe sunscreen, water shoes, and a dry bag for boat/cable car days.
  • Mobile data helps a lot for transport and translation — Saily eSIM with code CARL10 is included in the video notes.
