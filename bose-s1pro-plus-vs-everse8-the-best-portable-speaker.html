Bose S1 Pro Plus vs Everse 8

Which portable PA system should you buy?

Welcome back to Carl Tomich Tech Reviews! Today we’re veering slightly off the path of video gear and diving into something just as crucial — audio. Because let’s face it: great visuals mean nothing if your sound sucks.

We’re putting two top contenders head-to-head: the Bose S1 Pro Plus and the Electro-Voice Everse 8. Both are compact, battery-powered PA speakers — perfect for busking, solo gigs, small events, or even just jamming in the park.

Speaker Size: Does Bigger Mean Better?

  • Everse 8: Packs an 8-inch woofer
  • Bose S1 Pro Plus: Slightly smaller at 6.5 inches

Now, bigger drivers usually mean deeper bass and more volume. But it’s not just about size — we’re also looking at clarity, warmth, and how each speaker fills a space. You’ll hear it in the video — but spoiler alert: the difference might surprise you.

Inputs & Features: Aux Wars

Both speakers offer dual combo XLR/TRS inputs, but here’s a key difference:

  • Bose S1 Pro Plus: Features a 3.5mm aux input
  • Everse 8: Uses a 1/4" (6.35mm) aux jack

It’s a small detail, but depending on your gear — phone, tablet, mixer — it can save you from scrambling for adapters at the worst time.

Battery Life & Portability

Both units are battery-powered, with enough juice to get you through a few solid sets.

The Bose S1 Pro Plus has a sleek, ultra-portable design and wireless transmitter support. The Everse 8 is slightly bulkier, but offers that beefier low-end thanks to the larger driver.

Wireless Wins?

The Bose comes bundled with wireless transmitters that slot right into the speaker — great for performers who hate cable clutter. It’s a premium feature, and yeah, it bumps the price, but if wireless is your vibe, this might be a game-changer.

Price: Where’s the Value?

  • Bose S1 Pro Plus: ~$1300 AUD
  • Everse 8: ~$900–$1000 AUD

You're paying a premium for Bose’s wireless features and sleek form factor. The Everse 8 gives you a solid punch at a more affordable price.

🎧 Sound Test: Guitar, Vocals, Bass

In the video below, we A/B test both speakers using guitar and bass tracks. Everything’s recorded under the same conditions and volume, so you can hear the difference for yourself — not just take my word for it.

To my ears? The Everse 8 wins on depth, while the Bose has better midrange clarity — but it’ll depend on your use case. Gigging solo acoustic? Go Bose. Pumping backing tracks or DJing? Maybe the Everse is your pick.

My Final Thoughts

Both of these speakers are excellent choices. It really comes down to what you value more:

  • Portability and wireless convenience? → Bose S1 Pro Plus
  • Bigger sound at a better price? → Everse 8
