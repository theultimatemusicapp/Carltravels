New in Hanoi

Lotte World Aquarium Hanoi

Take a deep dive into Hanoi’s most exciting family attraction inside the gleaming Lotte Mall Westlake. From giant rays gliding above to hands-on pearl workshops, this aquarium delivers a full day of urban adventure.

🎥 Aquarium & Mall Tour

Join us as we explore the brand-new Lotte Mall Westlake and its show-stopping Lotte World Aquarium — complete with sharks, rays, jellyfish, and plenty of kid-friendly fun.

Why We Love It

Hanoi finally has a world-class aquarium to match its vibrant food and street culture. With over 30,000 marine creatures across 400 species, every turn reveals something new — from the neon coral reef tunnel to gliding sand tiger sharks.

  • Massive underwater tunnel with sharks, rays, and schooling fish.
  • Pearl harvesting workshop where you can turn a real pearl into jewelry.
  • Interactive feeding stations and touch pools that are perfect for kids.
  • Sea Adventure zone with jellyfish blooms, sea turtles, and sand tiger sharks.

Plan Your Visit

You’ll find the aquarium on the B1 floor of Lotte Mall Westlake in Ba Đình District. Tickets start at approximately 290,000 VND for adults and 200,000 VND for children, with combo packages available for mall experiences. Weekday visits around lunchtime or early afternoon mean smaller crowds and shorter lines.

Getting There

Grab a taxi or ride-hailing service straight to Lotte Mall Westlake. There’s ample basement parking for cars and bikes.

Best Time

Aim for weekday lunches or early afternoons to enjoy the tunnels before the after-school rush.

Tickets

Adults: ~290,000 VND
Kids: ~200,000 VND
Prices may change seasonally.

Make a Day of It

When you’re finished at the aquarium, wander the mall’s curated boutiques, catch a film at the cinema, or grab dinner overlooking West Lake. Lotte Mall Westlake is a full lifestyle destination, so give yourself time to explore.

  • Gourmet dining court and lakeside cafés
  • CGV cinema with luxury seating
  • Korean and international fashion brands

Quick Facts

  • Neighborhood: Tây Hồ & Ba Đình border
  • Family Friendly: Absolutely — stroller access & nursing rooms on site
  • Accessibility: Elevators connect every floor; ramps inside exhibits
  • Time Needed: 2–3 hours for aquarium, 4–5 with mall exploration

Tips Before You Go

  • Pre-book online on weekends to skip the ticket queue.
  • Bring a polarizing filter if you’re filming — the tunnel glass is pristine but reflective.
  • Shows and feedings run hourly — check the digital boards as you enter.
  • Kids can stamp an explorer passport at different zones — it’s a fun way to keep them engaged.

Whether you’re pairing it with a date night, keeping the kids entertained, or just cooling off after a morning of Hanoi street food, the Lotte World Aquarium is a refreshing addition to the city’s must-do list. It also fits neatly into the top 10 things to do in Hanoi loop.
