INSIDE THE EXPERIENCE

Where Emotion Meets the Plate

At Khuê, food is more than flavour—it’s a narrative. Every dish is crafted with inspiration drawn from emotion, memory, and connection. The menu moves from humble street food roots to meticulously plated fine dining creations, capturing the warmth of home cooking with the imagination of modern cuisine.

This film explores the heart behind Khuê’s culinary philosophy. We stepped into the kitchen, spoke with the passionate minds who bring each dish to life, and captured the intimate dining atmosphere that has made Khuê one of Hanoi’s most talked-about restaurants.

If your dinner plans lead into a full evening out, the Hanoi by night story is a perfect next stop.

Whether you’re a traveller chasing the city’s best dining experiences or a local searching for something special, Khuê offers a journey that touches the heart as much as the palate.

Why Khuê is Making Waves

Stories on Every Plate

From childhood memories to global inspirations, each course is designed to evoke a feeling—connecting diners through shared experiences.

Elevated Vietnamese Flavours

Classic northern ingredients meet modern techniques, resulting in a fresh take on Vietnamese cuisine without losing its soul.

Culinary Conversations

Sit at the chef’s counter and you’ll hear the stories behind the menu—why each dish matters and what inspired it.

Cinematic Atmosphere

Soft lighting, refined decor, and intimate details create a visual mood that feels both elevated and welcoming.
