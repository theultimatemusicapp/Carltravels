Hanoi: Street Eats, Train Tracks & Ha Long Bay

Dive into the chaos and charm of Vietnam's capital—where ancient temples meet neon bars and pho is a way of life.

See the Itinerary Read the Guide

Welcome to Hanoi

Hanoi isn’t a city you visit—it’s one you feel. It’s the clatter of chopsticks at a pho stall, the roar of scooters at dusk, and the laughter spilling from a bia hoi corner. It’s unpolished, chaotic, and absolutely alive.

Whether you’re sipping coffee in the expat enclave of Tay Ho or losing yourself in the Old Quarter’s maze, Hanoi rewards the curious. This 7-day itinerary covers everything from train street thrills to the misty cliffs of Ha Long Bay.

Where to Stay

Tay Ho (West Lake)

Expat hub, quieter, rooftop bars, lake views. Social but chill.

Old Quarter

Heart of the action. Chaotic, charming, steps from street food and Beer Street.

Expect $20–$60 USD/night for mid-range stays.

7-Day Hanoi Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Banh Mi 25

Dive into the sensory overload. Check in, then hit Banh Mi 25 (Hang Ca st) for a crispy pork belly sandwich ($2.50). Stroll Hoan Kiem Lake at twilight and end with a cheap draft at Beer Street.

Day 2: Train Street & French Colonial

Find a trackside café at Train Street (Tran Phu) and watch the steel giant squeeze past (~7 PM). Earlier, sip egg coffee at Cong Caphe and admire the Opera House.

Day 3: Temple of Literature & Tay Ho

Soak up history at the Temple of Literature. Spend the afternoon in Tay Ho—sip bia hoi (40 cents!) by West Lake and watch the sunset.

Mid-Trip Check-in

Day 4. Spent $22 on street food. Regret: Skipped the museums.

Day 4: Pho & Street Food Tour

Breakfast: Pho Thin (13 Lo Duc)—rich beef broth perfection ($2). Afternoon: DIY street food crawl (Bun Cha, Nem Ran, Che) in the Old Quarter.

Day 5: Culture & Rooftop Views

Visit Hoa Lo Prison ("Hanoi Hilton") for a heavy dose of history. Cap the day at Summit Lounge (Sofitel) or Top of Hanoi for panoramic city sips.

Days 6 & 7: Ha Long Bay

Escape to the emerald waters. Book a 2-day cruise to kayak lagoons and sleep under the limestone karsts. It's touristic but unmissable magic.

Packing List

  • Light, breathable clothes (Humidity is real)
  • Rain poncho (Essential Jun–Aug)
  • Solid walking shoes (Uneven sidewalks)
  • Modest wear for temples (Cover shoulders/knees)

Local Smarts

  • Crossing Roads: Walk slow and steady. Do not stop.
  • Cash: Keep small bills for street vendors.
  • Grab App: Essential for cheap rides and food.
  • Bia Hoi: Drink it. It's cheap, fresh, and social.

Hanoi at a Glance

  • Budget: $20–$60/day
  • Traffic: Chaotic Fun
  • Must-Eat: Bun Cha
  • Beer: ~$0.40 USD
