Da Nang: Beaches & Hidden Gems

Discover Da Nang’s golden shores, the mystic Marble Mountains, and the surreal spectacle of Ba Na Hills.

See the Itinerary Read the Guide

Welcome to Da Nang

Da Nang welcomed me with its laid-back beach vibes, stunning Marble Mountains, and the quirky charm of Ba Na Hills. This coastal gem blends affordability with adventure, from golden sands to cultural treasures.

My first day soaking up the sun and exploring local markets set the tone for an unforgettable adventure. It's Vietnam’s rising star for a reason.

3-Day Da Nang Itinerary

Day 1: Beaches & City Vibes

Start your morning relaxing at My Khe Beach with a fresh coconut. In the afternoon, explore the Dragon Bridge and Han River.

Evening: Dine at local seafood stalls on An Thuong street for the freshest catch.

Day 2: Marble Mountains & Hoi An

Morning: Hike the Marble Mountains to explore ancient caves and pagodas.

Afternoon/Evening: Take a 30-min taxi to Hoi An. Wander the ancient town and enjoy a lantern-lit dinner before returning to Da Nang.

Day 3: Ba Na Hills Adventure

Dedicate the full day to Ba Na Hills. Ride the world's longest cable car and walk the famous Golden Bridge.

Evening: Chill at a beachfront bar on My Khe to wrap up your trip.

Travel Tips

  • Best Time: Feb–May or Sep–Nov. Avoid the rainy season (Oct–Dec).
  • Language: English is common in tourist areas. A simple “Xin chào” (Hello) goes a long way.
  • Money: Cash (VND) is king for street vendors. Cards work in malls/hotels.
  • Transport: Use Grab for cheap, reliable rides. Taxis work too.
  • Respect: Dress modestly at temples (shoulders and knees covered).

Pro Tip

Bargain gently at markets. It's part of the culture, but keep it friendly and fair.

Where to Stay

Da Nang offers incredible value. Staying near My Khe Beach gives you the best of both worlds: ocean views and city access.

Getting Around & In

Getting Around

  • Grab/Taxi: 50k–150k VND ($2–$6) per trip.
  • Motorbike Rental: 100k–200k VND ($4–$8) per day. (Wear a helmet!)
  • Walking: My Khe Beach and the immediate downtown are walkable.

Getting There

  • Airport (DAD): Only 10 mins to downtown (~100k VND taxi).
  • Train/Bus: Scenic train from Hue is famous. Buses are cheap ($2–$4) from Hoi An.

Final Thoughts

Da Nang feels like a hidden gem—affordable, beautiful, and less crowded than the big cities. The Marble Mountains awed me, and Ba Na Hills was a bizarre but fun adventure.
