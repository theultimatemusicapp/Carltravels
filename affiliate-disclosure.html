Affiliate Disclosure

Some pages on Carl Travels contain affiliate links. If you click one of those links and buy something, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

The short version: affiliate links help fund the travel guides, filming, editing time, hosting, and gear testing behind this site. I only want these links on pages where they make practical sense for the reader.

How Recommendations Are Chosen

Most recommendations come from things I have used while travelling, filming, editing, or living abroad. That includes camera gear, travel cards, eSIMs, editing tools, backpacks, microphones, lights, and services that make travel logistics easier.

When I have not personally used something long-term, I try to make that clear and keep the recommendation practical rather than pretending it is a lifetime-tested favourite.
