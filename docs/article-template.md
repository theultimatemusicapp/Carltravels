# Carl Tomich Article Template

Use this structure for major rewrites and new posts. The goal is not to sound polished. The goal is to sound useful, specific, and first-hand.

## 1. SEO fields

- **Title:** Specific, blunt, and searchable.
- **Meta description:** One sentence on who it is for, what it covers, and why Carl has firsthand context.
- **Canonical:** Final preferred URL only.
- **Category:** One of: Travel Filmmaking, Living Abroad, Creator Gear, Documentary Projects, YouTube Strategy, Travel Reality / Honest Guides.

## 2. Opening hook

Start with a direct observation, not a generic welcome.

Bad: “Whether you’re planning a trip or considering moving abroad…”

Better: “Hanoi is cheap until you choose the wrong apartment, the wrong visa plan, or the wrong month to arrive.”

## 3. Personal context

Explain why Carl can write it:

- where he was;
- what he was filming or testing;
- how long he stayed or used the product;
- what went wrong;
- what the reader should know before copying him.

## 4. Original media block

Add at least one of these near the top:

- embedded YouTube video;
- original photo with descriptive alt text;
- route map screenshot;
- gear photo;
- receipt/cost table;
- short behind-the-scenes note.

## 5. Main answer first

Give the useful verdict before the long story:

- Is it worth it?
- Who is it for?
- Who should skip it?
- What does it cost?
- What would Carl do differently next time?

## 6. Practical sections

Use concrete H2s:

- Cost breakdown
- What worked
- What annoyed me
- Mistakes to avoid
- Gear / apps / transport used
- Filming notes, if relevant
- Final verdict

## 7. Internal links

Add a “Read next” block with:

- one category hub link;
- two related articles;
- one film/project page when relevant.

## 8. Affiliate/product opportunities

Only add affiliate links where they fit the reader’s actual next step:

- creator gear reviews;
- eSIM / travel card pages;
- camera and laptop posts;
- booking/logistics pages.

Include a plain disclosure and make the review useful without the link.

## 9. Author credibility block

End with a short author note:

“Carl Tomich is a documentary filmmaker and travel creator who has filmed projects across Europe, Australia, and Asia, including Busking for Berlin, A Sail Untold, Busk Life, and a current martial arts documentary in production.”
