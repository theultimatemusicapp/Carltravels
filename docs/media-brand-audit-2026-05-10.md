# Carl Tomich Media Brand Audit — 2026-05-10

## Positioning to use everywhere

**Documentary filmmaker Carl Tomich explores travel, filmmaking, creator gear, YouTube strategy, and the realities of building a life abroad.**

This should replace the loose “Carl Travels” travel-blog frame. The site can still keep the domain, but the publication should feel like it is run by a filmmaker with a camera, a life overseas, and a working creator business — not a generic destination content farm.

## Site structure to keep

Primary navigation should be short and editorial:

1. **Films** — homepage filmography and documentary identity.
2. **Work** — client/portfolio proof.
3. **Current Doc** — the martial arts documentary in production.
4. **Field Notes** — all written publishing.
5. **Creator Gear** — camera, audio, drone, editing, laptop, travel tech.
6. **Honest Guides** — travel logistics, costs, visas, scams, route guides.
7. **About** — trust, author credibility, channels, IMDb, contact routes.

## AdSense readiness fixes before reapplying

1. Keep legal/trust pages crawlable: `about.html`, `contact.html`, `privacy-policy.html`, `terms-and-conditions.html`.
2. Remove or noindex utility, duplicate, and placeholder pages before submitting.
3. Remove generic travel-blog claims unless supported by first-hand photos/video/prices.
4. Add author blocks to major articles: Carl Tomich, documentary filmmaker, years filming/traveling, YouTube/IMDb links.
5. Add original media near the top of high-value posts: embedded YouTube video, personal photo, route screenshot, receipts, or location notes.
6. Add internal “Read next” blocks on every article using the six editorial lanes.
7. Avoid ad-heavy or affiliate-heavy first screens on thin pages.
8. Rewrite short affiliate posts so the review would still be useful if every affiliate link disappeared.
9. Keep category labels consistent; do not mix “travel blog”, “nomad tips”, “destination guide”, and “portfolio” randomly.
10. Submit only after the indexable site has a clear purpose: filmmaker-led field notes about travel, gear, YouTube, and life abroad.

## First 10 implementation changes

1. Replace homepage positioning with the new media-brand statement.

8. Rewrite top Vietnam/living-abroad posts with more first-person proof, original images, and practical next steps.
9. Add article template sections to new and rewritten articles.
10. Build a stronger internal-link system: every article links up to its category hub and sideways to two related posts.
