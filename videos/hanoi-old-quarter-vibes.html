Transcript Highlights

We start with hyperlapse footage weaving through the 36 streets while I narrate how each lane is still named after the craft once sold there.

Midway through I sit curbside with a bowl of bun cha, breaking down prices and etiquette for plastic-stool dining. Ambient sizzling and street musicians provide the soundtrack.

The closing section slows down at a bia hoi corner where locals toast with fresh beer. I talk about responsible drinking, safety tips for night markets, and my favorite alleyway dessert.
