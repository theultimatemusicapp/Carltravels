Transcript Highlights

Nhi opens the episode at the lakeside entrance, explaining how the pagoda once stood on the Red River before moving to West Lake for protection.

Mid-tour, we pause beneath the Bodhi tree gifted by India’s Prime Minister Nehru. Nhi shares the symbolism of its heart-shaped leaves and daily offerings.

The episode closes inside the main shrine, where Nhi discusses incense etiquette, prayer bead rituals, and the meaning behind the golden Amitabha statues stacked in the 11-story stupa.

Hanoi • West Lake

Trấn Quốc Pagoda Guided Tour

Experience Hanoi’s most storied temple with insider commentary from Nhi, a proud Hanoian. Learn the rituals, symbols, and best photography spots during this peaceful walk-through.
